How the Strategy Works

The SMA Crossover is one of the simplest and most widely used algorithmic trading strategies.

  • Buy Signal: When the Fast SMA crosses above the Slow SMA (bullish crossover).
  • Sell Signal: When the Fast SMA crosses below the Slow SMA (bearish crossover).
  • The strategy stays in cash when no position is open.
  • This simulation uses randomly generated price data that mimics real market volatility.

Try different parameter combinations to see how they affect performance. Shorter periods = more signals (and more noise). Longer periods = fewer, stronger signals.
